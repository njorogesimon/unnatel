Unnascan

Unnascan is an innovative QR-based payment platform designed to simplify and accelerate mobile money transactions between businesses and customers. The system enables merchants to generate unique QR codes linked to their registered mobile numbers, till or paybill numbers, allowing customers to make payments quickly and securely by simply scanning a QR code with their smartphone.

Unnascan supports phone number-based, till and paybill M-PESA payments, providing a seamless cashless payment experience for both merchants and customers enabling broader payment options for businesses of all sizes.

Key Features

  • Instant QR Code Payments – Customers can complete transactions within seconds by scanning a merchant's QR code.
  • Real-Time Payment Confirmation – Both merchants and customers receive immediate transaction status updates.
  • Secure M-PESA Integration – Payments are processed through trusted M-PESA infrastructure with verification mechanisms.
  • Fast & Convenient Checkout – Eliminates manual number entry and speeds up customer service.
  • Digital Payment Records – Maintains accurate transaction records for accountability and business management.
  • Merchants Embedding Wallets To QR Codes – Merchants embed their business phone numbers, till or paybill numbers to QR codes to receive transaction funds.
  • Print-Ready A4 QR Code Layout – Every generated QR code is automatically formatted on an A4 page with the business name clearly displayed for easy identification and immediate placement at counters.

Unnascan is committed to delivering a secure, efficient, and user-friendly digital payment ecosystem that empowers businesses to accept mobile payments with ease.

Unnapack is a comprehensive digital and web-based school management platform designed to streamline and digitize daily school operations. The system brings together student administration, fee management, academic records, staff management, transport coordination, and parent communication into a single integrated solution.

By automating routine administrative tasks, Unnapack reduces paperwork, improves record accuracy, and enhances communication between schools, parents, teachers, and administrators.

Key Features

  • Automated Fee Collection – Supports seamless school fee payments through M-PESA Paybill integration with automatic payment reconciliation.
  • Instant SMS Notifications & Receipts – Parents receive real-time payment confirmations, E-receipts through E-mail, and school updates via SMS.
  • Student Information Management – Maintains comprehensive student records including admissions, attendance, academic performance, and disciplinary history.
  • Examination & Academic Management – Facilitates exam setup, grading, report card generation, and performance analysis.
  • Staff & Transport Management – Organizes employee workflows and tracks transport routes, drivers, and student transportation records.
  • Parent & School Communication – Enhances engagement through timely notifications, announcements, and updates.
  • Digital Operations Dashboard – Provides real-time insights into finances, student data, and overall school operations.

Unnapack empowers schools to embrace digital transformation through a secure, reliable, and scalable platform.
